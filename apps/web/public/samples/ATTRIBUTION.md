# Sample image attribution

The four clinical photographs bundled with the demo come from Wikimedia Commons. They are
downscaled to 900px on the long edge and re-encoded as JPEG; no other alteration was made.
They are included so a reviewer can run the workflow without having to find a clinical
photograph first. They are illustrative samples, not a dataset, and nothing here was trained
on them.
